<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>x = 5, y = 3

</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: x</strong> = 25, y = 13
<strong>Output: </strong>26
<strong>Explanation: </strong>26 is the smallest number greater than 25 which is divisible by 13.
</span></pre>
